Ventnor Tarot

A photorealistic Rider–Waite–Smith deck set against the real shores, cliffs, and streets of Ventnor.

This is a full 78-card tarot deck that follows the Rider–Waite tradition, but every card starts from a real photograph taken in or around Ventnor — the cliffs, the sea, the streets, the gardens, the festivals. We add only the people and symbols each card needs, lit and scaled so they belong in the scene. The idea is a deck that feels local and lived-in, not like generic fantasy art.

  • Real backgrounds. Each card uses a genuine photo. We might crop it or tweak the light, but we don't invent or replace the place.
  • Faithful cards. The figures, objects, and layout follow the Rider–Waite card — nothing important gets left out.
  • Photorealistic additions. Anything we add is meant to look like it was there when the photo was taken, not painted or illustrated on top.
  • One consistent frame. Every card shares the same border, type, and finish.

Card Notes

Each finished card can carry its own notes: the local idea behind the image, how it translates Rider-Waite-Smith symbolism, and the public source images that are safe to share. Some cards already have full notes; the rest will fill in gradually as the deck records catch up with the finished artwork.

Source images are included only when they are suitable for publication. Private person-reference photographs and identifying names are left out by default.
